## 2026-10-01

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Seoul and Kyiv try to contain dispute over North Korean POW disclosure
- **What happened:** Ukraine's foreign minister described the dispute over public disclosure of two captured North Korean soldiers transferred to South Korea as a diplomatic misunderstanding and said Kyiv hoped to resolve it quickly. Seoul had expected confidentiality; Ukraine disputed that a secrecy agreement existed.
- **Why it matters:** The episode links the Russia-Ukraine war directly to Korean Peninsula security and tests Seoul-Kyiv relations at a time when North Korean military support for Russia is under growing scrutiny.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/ukrainian-minister-hopes-heal-misunderstanding-with-south-korea-media-reports-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `prisoner of war (POW)` / `전쟁포로` — tù binh chiến tranh; `confidentiality agreement` / `비공개 합의` — thỏa thuận bảo mật; `diplomatic dispute` / `외교 갈등` — tranh chấp ngoại giao.

### 🌍 Global / Other

#### Oil tankers are struck in the Strait of Hormuz
- **What happened:** Three Liberian-flagged oil tankers were hit by projectiles while transiting the Strait of Hormuz, according to maritime-risk reporting cited by Reuters.
- **Why it matters:** Hormuz is a critical global energy chokepoint. Security incidents there can quickly affect oil prices, shipping insurance and energy-importing economies such as South Korea and Vietnam.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-tankers-hit-by-projectiles-hormuz-strait-tuesday-marisks-says-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `maritime chokepoint` / `해상 요충지` — điểm nghẽn hàng hải chiến lược; `shipping risk` / `해운 위험` — rủi ro vận tải biển; `energy security` / `에너지 안보` — an ninh năng lượng.

## 2026-10-02

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### North Korea rejects Seoul's accusation over the DMZ mine blast
- **What happened:** North Korea rejected South Korea's claim that North Korean mines caused the Sept. 21 blast in the Demilitarized Zone that seriously injured South Korean personnel. Seoul and the United Nations Command had said the incident violated the armistice.
- **Why it matters:** The dispute combines actual casualties, explosive hazards and competing narratives over the border, making it more consequential than routine rhetorical exchanges.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/north-korea-rejects-souths-accusation-over-dmz-mine-blast-kcna-says-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `landmine` / `지뢰` — mìn; `Demilitarized Zone (DMZ)` / `비무장지대` — khu phi quân sự; `armistice` / `정전협정` — hiệp định đình chiến.

#### President Lee warns Ukraine of further measures in POW dispute
- **What happened:** President Lee Jae Myung warned that Seoul could take unspecified additional measures if Kyiv continued to reject what South Korea says was a confidentiality agreement concerning the transfer of two North Korean prisoners.
- **Why it matters:** A dispute that began over disclosure has escalated to the head-of-state level, potentially complicating South Korea's relationship with Ukraine and its broader positioning on the Russia-Ukraine war.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-koreas-lee-threatens-action-against-ukraine-over-transfer-north-korean-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `additional measures` / `추가 조치` — biện pháp bổ sung; `bilateral relations` / `양자관계` — quan hệ song phương; `North Korean troops` / `북한군` — quân đội Triều Tiên.

## 2026-10-03

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### North Korea confirms intermediate-range strategic missile drill
- **What happened:** North Korea said it conducted an intermediate-range strategic missile test overseen by Kim Jong Un. South Korea said the missile traveled more than 700 km toward the sea; Pyongyang claimed advanced trajectory and AI-related capabilities.
- **Why it matters:** The test adds another layer of tension after the DMZ mine dispute and reinforces concerns about North Korea's efforts to improve survivability and interception resistance. Technical claims made by Pyongyang have not all been independently verified.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/north-korea-says-it-conducted-intermediate-range-strategic-missile-drill-2026-10-03/)
- **Keywords:** `intermediate-range ballistic missile` / `중거리 탄도미사일` — tên lửa đạn đạo tầm trung; `hypersonic weapon` / `극초음속 무기` — vũ khí siêu vượt âm; `deterrence` / `억제력` — năng lực răn đe.

## 2026-10-04

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Weekend security focus shifts to the missile's maneuvering claims
- **What happened:** By early Oct. 4 KST, follow-up reporting focused on North Korea's claim that the missile could alter its low-altitude flight path and use AI-related technology. South Korea confirmed the launch but the more advanced technical claims remained North Korean assertions.
- **Why it matters:** If such maneuverability were validated, it could complicate missile tracking and interception. For now, the important distinction is between confirmed launch data and unverified capability claims.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/north-koreas-kim-yo-jong-says-intermediate-range-missile-test-was-conducted-2026-10-03/)
- **Keywords:** `maneuverable trajectory` / `기동형 궤도` — quỹ đạo cơ động; `missile defense` / `미사일 방어` — phòng thủ tên lửa; `independent verification` / `독립 검증` — xác minh độc lập.
