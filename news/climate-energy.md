## 2026-10-01

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### Ho Chi Minh City remains at risk of severe flooding through Oct. 6
- **What happened:** After heavy rain and tidal conditions flooded parts of Ho Chi Minh City, forecasters warned of continued afternoon and evening downpours, with stronger rainfall expected around Oct. 5-6. Authorities were monitoring 159 recurring flood-risk locations.
- **Why it matters:** The event highlights how extreme rainfall, high tides, drainage capacity and construction-related obstructions can combine to create urban flood risk.
- **Sources:** [VnExpress International](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/news/environment/ho-chi-minh-city-faces-severe-rain-and-flooding-risks-through-oct-6-5127129.html)
- **Keywords:** `urban flooding` / `도시 침수` — ngập đô thị; `drainage capacity` / `배수 능력` — năng lực thoát nước; `high tide` / `만조` — triều cường.

### 🌍 Global / Other

#### China suspends most October fuel exports
- **What happened:** Chinese refiners suspended most oil-product exports for October as authorities prioritized domestic inventories.
- **Why it matters:** China is a major regional fuel supplier. Lower exports can tighten diesel and gasoline availability across Asia and put upward pressure on logistics and inflation.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/chinese-refiners-suspend-oct-fuel-exports-petrochina-cancels-cargoes-sources-say-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `fuel exports` / `연료 수출` — xuất khẩu nhiên liệu; `inventory` / `재고` — tồn kho; `regional supply` / `역내 공급` — nguồn cung khu vực.

#### Security incidents in Hormuz push energy risk higher
- **What happened:** Three oil tankers were reported hit by projectiles while transiting the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Why it matters:** Disruption risk at one of the world's most important oil chokepoints can quickly feed into crude prices, insurance premiums and Asian import costs.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-tankers-hit-by-projectiles-hormuz-strait-tuesday-marisks-says-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `Strait of Hormuz` / `호르무즈 해협` — eo biển Hormuz; `oil chokepoint` / `원유 수송 요충지` — điểm nghẽn vận chuyển dầu; `risk premium` / `위험 프리미엄` — phần bù rủi ro.

## 2026-10-02

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### Nghi Son refinery secures crude to run at 125% capacity through November
- **What happened:** Vietnam's largest refinery said it had secured enough crude, mostly from Kuwait, to operate at around 125% of designed capacity through the end of November.
- **Why it matters:** Stable refinery throughput lowers the risk of domestic fuel shortages at a time when global energy markets remain volatile.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/vietnams-nghi-son-refinery-secures-crude-supplies-run-125-capacity-through-end-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `refinery throughput` / `정유공장 처리량` — sản lượng xử lý của nhà máy lọc dầu; `crude supply` / `원유 공급` — nguồn cung dầu thô; `fuel security` / `연료 안보` — an ninh nhiên liệu.

### 🇺🇸 United States / 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Seoul disputes Trump's claim about an $8.4B oil-recovery project
- **What happened:** President Donald Trump said a U.S.-South Korea deal included $8.4 billion for enhanced oil recovery, while a South Korean industry official said the project was not part of Seoul's understanding of the agreement.
- **Why it matters:** The disagreement shows that large bilateral energy-investment packages can remain politically announced before commercial scope and legal commitments are fully aligned.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-says-us-deal-with-south-korea-includes-84-billion-for-oil-recovery-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `enhanced oil recovery (EOR)` / `석유회수증진` — tăng cường thu hồi dầu; `investment commitment` / `투자 약정` — cam kết đầu tư; `commercial feasibility` / `사업성` — tính khả thi thương mại.

### 🌍 Global / Other

#### G7 agrees to release 100M barrels of emergency fuel stocks
- **What happened:** G7 countries agreed to release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude oil from emergency reserves after energy-market disruptions and rising fuel prices.
- **Why it matters:** Strategic stock releases are designed to cushion short-term supply shocks, but they do not solve the underlying geopolitical risks affecting shipping and production.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/europe-discussing-plan-diesel-stock-releases-after-us-pressure-source-says-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `strategic reserve` / `전략 비축유` — dự trữ chiến lược; `supply shock` / `공급 충격` — cú sốc nguồn cung; `diesel stocks` / `경유 비축량` — dự trữ dầu diesel.

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### HCMC enters another high-risk rainfall window
- **What happened:** The Oct. 4 batch keeps the city's flood warning active because forecasters expected rainfall to strengthen on Oct. 5-6 after earlier flooding exposed drainage bottlenecks.
