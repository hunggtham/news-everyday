## 2026-10-01

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### SEMIEXPO 2026 launch highlights Vietnam's semiconductor ambitions
- **What happened:** Vietnam's National Innovation Centre and SEMI Southeast Asia launched preparations for SEMIEXPO Vietnam 2026. Officials highlighted targets including at least 100 semiconductor design companies, one small-scale fab, 10 packaging/testing facilities and more than 50,000 semiconductor engineers and graduates by 2030.
- **Why it matters:** Vietnam is trying to move deeper into the semiconductor value chain instead of remaining mainly an electronics assembly base. Talent, supplier capability, infrastructure and international standards are the key bottlenecks.
- **Sources:** [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1801023/semiexpo-2026-to-strengthen-viet-nam-s-semiconductor-value-chain-global-links.html)
- **Keywords:** `semiconductor value chain` / `반도체 가치사슬` — chuỗi giá trị bán dẫn; `fab` / `반도체 생산공장` — nhà máy chế tạo chip; `packaging and testing` / `패키징·테스트` — đóng gói và kiểm thử.

## 2026-10-02

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### iTECH EXPO puts chips, robotics and infrastructure at the center
- **What happened:** iTECH EXPO 2026 in Ho Chi Minh City closed after showcasing more than 1,000 products and solutions. Hardware, network infrastructure, robotics, chips and integrated circuits accounted for roughly 45% of exhibition space, a notable shift from the software-heavy focus of previous years.
- **Why it matters:** Vietnam's digital-industry agenda is broadening from software adoption toward hardware, semiconductor ecosystems and technology commercialization.
- **Sources:** [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/Sci-Tech/1800954/international-technology-exhibition-opens-with-focus-on-semiconductors-and-ai.html)
- **Keywords:** `technology commercialization` / `기술 사업화` — thương mại hóa công nghệ; `integrated circuit (IC)` / `집적회로` — mạch tích hợp; `robotics` / `로보틱스` — công nghệ robot.

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Financial regulator calls emergency meeting after bank cyberattacks
- **What happened:** South Korea's Financial Services Commission convened banks and financial institutions after cyber intrusions affecting major lenders. Hana Bank said personal information of 89 customers was exposed, while Shinhan, KB and Woori also reported incidents.
- **Why it matters:** Highly digitized banking makes cybersecurity a financial-stability issue. Even when account balances are not stolen, leaked identity data can increase phishing, smishing and account-takeover risk.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/south-korea-finance-regulator-holds-emergency-meeting-over-bank-hacks-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `data breach` / `개인정보 유출` — rò rỉ dữ liệu cá nhân; `cyber intrusion` / `사이버 침해` — xâm nhập mạng; `financial cybersecurity` / `금융 보안` — an ninh mạng tài chính.

### 🇺🇸 United States

#### Apple adds clearer controls around AI access to Mac data
- **What happened:** Apple said future macOS changes would more clearly flag AI applications asking for user data access after complaints involving Meta's Muse.
- **Why it matters:** Operating systems are becoming the enforcement layer between local user data and increasingly autonomous AI assistants.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-says-it-will-flag-ai-requests-mac-data-after-metas-muse-draws-complaints-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `OS-level permission` / `운영체제 권한` — quyền ở cấp hệ điều hành; `local data` / `로컬 데이터` — dữ liệu cục bộ; `AI assistant` / `AI 비서` — trợ lý AI.

## 2026-10-03

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Bank-hack investigations become a broader sector-wide security review
- **What happened:** The investigation into intrusions at major Korean banks remained active, with regulators pushing institutions to review access controls and defensive systems rather than treating the cases as isolated IT incidents.
- **Why it matters:** The Korean financial sector's reliance on mobile banking, APIs and digital identity raises the potential systemic cost of weak authentication or compromised customer data.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/south-korea-finance-regulator-holds-emergency-meeting-over-bank-hacks-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `access control` / `접근통제` — kiểm soát truy cập; `incident response` / `침해사고 대응` — ứng phó sự cố; `digital identity` / `디지털 신원` — danh tính số.
