## 2026-10-02

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Korea abolishes the old Prosecutors' Office after 78 years
- **What happened:** South Korea launched a new Public Prosecution Service focused on indictment/prosecution and a Serious Crimes Investigation Agency responsible for major investigations, replacing the old prosecution structure after 78 years.
- **Why it matters:** This is a structural change to Korean criminal justice: investigative and prosecutorial powers are deliberately separated to reduce the concentration of authority once held by prosecutors. The practical test will be coordination, staffing and investigative capability during the transition.
- **Sources:** [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/korea-launches-scaleddown-prosecution-new-investigation-agency-in-major-reform/12902427), [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/f36fb808-5942-4589-a201-857598a8dfc3)
- **Keywords:** `investigation` / `수사` — điều tra; `indictment/prosecution` / `기소·공소` — truy tố; `수사·기소 분리` — tách quyền điều tra và truy tố; `중대범죄수사청` — Cơ quan Điều tra Tội phạm Nghiêm trọng.

## 2026-10-03

### 🇺🇸 United States

#### Trump administration formalizes a voluntary AI-safety approach
- **What happened:** The White House promoted a voluntary AI-safety framework with major technology companies instead of a binding enforcement regime.
- **Why it matters:** The policy choice shapes how the U.S. balances AI competitiveness, innovation and public-safety concerns, and contrasts with more rules-based approaches elsewhere.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/public-fears-ai-grow-trump-digs-voluntary-safeguards-2026-10-03/)
- **Keywords:** `self-regulation` / `자율규제` — tự điều chỉnh; `AI safety` / `AI 안전` — an toàn AI; `binding regulation` / `구속력 있는 규제` — quy định có tính bắt buộc.

#### New White House AI task force links AI policy more closely to national security
- **What happened:** Reuters reported that intelligence chief Jay Clayton would lead a White House AI task force charged with assessing risks and opportunities and reporting within 120 days.
- **Why it matters:** It signals that AI governance is increasingly being treated as a national-security issue, not only an economic or technology-policy topic.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/jay-clayton-lead-trumps-ai-task-force-deliver-report-120-days-wsj-reports-2026-10-03/)
- **Keywords:** `AI governance` / `AI 거버넌스` — quản trị AI; `national-security policy` / `국가안보 정책` — chính sách an ninh quốc gia; `federal task force` / `연방 태스크포스` — tổ công tác liên bang.

## 2026-10-04

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### Prosecution reform enters its implementation phase
- **What happened:** By the Oct. 4 KST batch, South Korea's newly created investigative and prosecutorial bodies had begun operating, while leadership vacancies and implementation questions remained part of the transition.
- **Why it matters:** The reform's success will depend less on the legal redesign itself and more on whether the new agencies can preserve investigative quality, accountability and case continuity.
- **Sources:** [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/society/2026/10/04/acting-chiefs-fill-top-law-enforcement-posts-as-vacancies)
- **Keywords:** `institutional transition` / `제도 전환` — chuyển đổi thể chế; `acting chief` / `직무대행` — quyền lãnh đạo tạm thời; `case continuity` / `사건 연속성` — tính liên tục xử lý vụ án.
