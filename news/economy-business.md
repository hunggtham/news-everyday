## 2026-10-01

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### Pandora opens its largest jewellery factory in Vietnam
- **What happened:** Denmark's Pandora opened a roughly $150 million manufacturing facility in Ho Chi Minh City, described as its largest jewellery factory globally.
- **Why it matters:** The project is another example of manufacturing FDI moving into higher-value, skill-intensive production rather than only low-cost assembly.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/denmarks-pandora-opens-worlds-largest-jewellery-factory-vietnam-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `foreign direct investment (FDI)` / `외국인직접투자` — đầu tư trực tiếp nước ngoài; `manufacturing capacity` / `생산능력` — năng lực sản xuất; `value-added manufacturing` / `고부가가치 제조업` — sản xuất giá trị gia tăng cao.

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### September exports hit a record $120.9B on chip boom
- **What happened:** South Korean exports rose 83.5% year on year in September to a record $120.9 billion, with semiconductor sales driving the surge.
- **Why it matters:** AI-related memory demand is now large enough to materially lift national exports, corporate earnings and tax receipts, but it also increases Korea's dependence on the semiconductor cycle.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-september-exports-rise-835-yy-monthly-record-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `export growth` / `수출 증가` — tăng trưởng xuất khẩu; `trade surplus` / `무역흑자` — thặng dư thương mại; `semiconductor cycle` / `반도체 경기순환` — chu kỳ ngành bán dẫn.

### 🇺🇸 United States

#### Bond yields surge as Fed officials debate further tightening
- **What happened:** U.S. Treasury yields climbed to multi-decade highs amid renewed inflation concerns. Fed officials sent mixed signals, with Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan arguing that at least 50 basis points of further hikes could be needed while other policymakers favored waiting for more data.
- **Why it matters:** Higher U.S. yields raise global borrowing costs, support the dollar and can pressure Asian currencies and capital flows, including KRW and VND.
- **Sources:** [Reuters — bond selloff](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/why-are-world-bond-markets-selling-off-again-2026-10-01/), [Reuters — Fed Logan](https://www.reuters.com/world/feds-logan-calls-50-bps-or-more-rate-hikes-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `Treasury yield` / `미 국채 수익률` — lợi suất trái phiếu Kho bạc Mỹ; `basis point` / `베이시스포인트` — điểm cơ bản; `monetary tightening` / `통화긴축` — thắt chặt tiền tệ.

## 2026-10-02

### 🇰🇷 South Korea

#### September inflation eases to 2.9%
- **What happened:** South Korea's consumer inflation slowed to 2.9% year on year in September from 3.1% in August, matching expectations, although petroleum-related price pressure remained significant.
- **Why it matters:** The Bank of Korea must balance strong AI/chip-led growth against imported energy inflation and the effects of a 3% policy rate.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-september-inflation-eases-29-yy-matches-forecast-2026-10-01/)
- **Keywords:** `consumer price index (CPI)` / `소비자물가지수` — chỉ số giá tiêu dùng; `headline inflation` / `헤드라인 물가` — lạm phát toàn phần; `policy rate` / `기준금리` — lãi suất cơ bản.

### 🇺🇸 United States

#### Weak jobs data revive expectations for Fed patience
- **What happened:** Global markets rose after softer U.S. labor data increased expectations that the Federal Reserve could wait before tightening again, even as bond-market volatility remained high.
- **Why it matters:** The combination of slower labor momentum and persistent inflation pressure makes the U.S. rate path unusually uncertain, with direct consequences for equities, the dollar and global funding costs.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-wrapup-1-2026-10-02/)
- **Keywords:** `labor-market cooling` / `고용시장 둔화` — thị trường lao động hạ nhiệt; `rate expectations` / `금리 기대` — kỳ vọng lãi suất; `financial conditions` / `금융여건` — điều kiện tài chính.

## 2026-10-03

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

#### Q3 GDP grows 9.95%, fastest quarterly pace in four years
- **What happened:** Vietnam's economy expanded 9.95% year on year in the third quarter. September exports reached about $59.48 billion, while nine-month FDI disbursement rose and industrial output remained strong. Inflation also accelerated, and the year-to-date trade balance remained in deficit.
- **Why it matters:** Vietnam is showing very strong growth, but the policy challenge is shifting toward avoiding overheating, maintaining electricity supply and controlling inflation while still pursuing an ambitious annual growth target.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/vietnam-q3-economic-growth-up-995-yy-2026-10-03/)
- **Keywords:** `GDP growth` / `GDP 성장률` — tăng trưởng GDP; `trade deficit` / `무역적자` — nhập siêu; `economic overheating` / `경기 과열` — nền kinh tế tăng nóng.

### 🌍 Global / Other

#### AI investment boom raises questions about future returns
- **What happened:** Reuters analysis highlighted the enormous scale of AI infrastructure commitments and the revenue growth that hyperscalers and AI firms would need to generate to justify them.
- **Why it matters:** A sustained AI capex cycle can boost global industrial growth, but a gap between investment and realized productivity could eventually pressure valuations, credit markets and suppliers.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/ais-race-transform-world-before-money-runs-out-2026-10-03/)
- **Keywords:** `capital expenditure (capex)` / `설비투자` — chi tiêu vốn; `productivity gain` / `생산성 향상` — tăng năng suất; `valuation risk` / `밸류에이션 위험` — rủi ro định giá.
